Engineering Equations Used

Maintenance Cost Model

Ctotal = Cpreventive + Ccorrective + Copportunity

Where:
Cpreventive = Preventive maintenance cost
Ccorrective = Corrective maintenance cost
Copportunity = Opportunity cost due to downtime

Failure Rate (Weibull Distribution)

λ(t) = (β/η) × (t/η)β-1

Where:
β = Shape parameter (β > 1 for wear-out failures)
η = Scale parameter (characteristic life)
t = Operating time

Availability Calculation

A = MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR)

Where:
MTBF = Mean Time Between Failures
MTTR = Mean Time To Repair
A = System availability (0-1)

Optimal Maintenance Interval

Topt = η × [(Cf/Cp) × (β-1)]1/β

Where:
Cf = Cost of failure
Cp = Cost of preventive maintenance
Topt = Optimal maintenance interval

Thermal Stress Factor

σthermal = α × E × ΔT

Where:
α = Coefficient of thermal expansion
E = Young's modulus
ΔT = Temperature difference from ambient

Load Factor Impact

Flife = (Lrated/Lactual)3

Where:
Lrated = Rated load
Lactual = Actual operating load
Flife = Life factor (affects maintenance frequency)
